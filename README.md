# Nether Expansion

New threats for the Nether. Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3.

## The Underworld Watcher

An ancient stalker that guards the boundary between the Nether and something deeper. It appears in Nether
fortresses, standing at the far end of what you can see, and it reacts to whether you are looking at it.

- **Observing:** it stands still and stares. Its head turns slowly, but snaps to you when you look at it.
- **Closing in:** once you look away, it moves. It freezes whenever you look at it again, and waits just outside
  bright light. A heartbeat grows louder as it gets closer.
- **Hunting:** when its Hunt Pressure fills (or you attack it), it stops playing games. Looking at it now only
  stalls it for a moment.

Attacks, each with a warning and a counter:

- **Rift Claw:** a heavy slash after a short wind-up. Back off or strafe.
- **Nether Rift:** sinks into the ground and emerges a few blocks from you. Watch for rising smoke and embers.
- **Dread Pulse:** its eye turns violet, flashes white, then a dark wave rolls across the ground. Jump over it or
  get behind cover.
- **Grasp of the Deep:** the ground cracks and glows under you, then tendrils root you. Move off the cracks.
- **Abyssal Collapse:** a violet ring opens around it, then implodes. Get outside the ring before it does.
- **Shadow Step:** it sinks into a rift while motes gather and something whispers behind you, then it steps
  out there and slashes. Turn and step aside.

It is the miniboss that guards the way down. At 35% health it unveils: its mask cracks open, its eye splits in
three, and it mends back to full health. It also consumes your strength: every positive potion effect on nearby
players is drained away into it. Each one makes it stronger (+5% damage and +1 armor, up to ten), and it keeps
any Strength, Speed, Resistance, Regeneration, Absorption or Health Boost it takes for up to 30 seconds. Save your
potions for after it unveils. From then on it hits harder, takes less damage, moves faster, and
attacks more often.

- **False Sight:** two copies mirror its movement. Only the real one can hurt you, and only the real one has a
  heartbeat.
- **World Rend:** a glowing crack opens toward you and erupts a moment later.
- **Unblinking:** it charges a beam while staring at you. Break line of sight to interrupt it and stagger it.
- **Shard Rain:** glowing circles mark the ground around you, and obsidian shards come down on them a moment
  later. Stay out of the circles.
- **Shadow Step** strikes three times in a row, from different sides.

If it lands a killing blow, you do not just die. It walks up to you, lifts you by the neck, and your screen fades
to black before the end. A friend who hits it hard enough while it holds you makes it let go. A totem of undying
still saves you.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API
- GeckoLib 5.5.7 or newer

## Building

Needs Java 25.

```
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar is written to `build/libs`. To try it in a development client, run `./gradlew runClient`.

## Assets

The Underworld Watcher's model, animations and textures are generated by `art/gen_underworld_watcher.py`
(run it from the project root). `art/underworld_watcher.bbmodel` is a Blockbench project for viewing the model.

The spawn egg texture is recolored from the vanilla enderman spawn egg, and the sounds reuse vanilla sound files,
so those parts belong to Mojang.
